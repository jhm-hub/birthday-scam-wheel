A FULL ROUND

The third press reveals one of the enabled birthday games. The earlier amounts stay on the bill.
Use the large button and any choices that appear. Each game builds a modest-looking quote,
then reveals the birthday catch and shows the exact added charge. The end card shows the
whole round bill. The popper waits for Pull the cord; other catches play automatically.

THE TEN BIRTHDAY CATCHES
1. Just One Slice: Spin for a slice price. The catch: Faith sells the whole eight-slice cake. The cake opens up and all eight slices are charged at that price.
2. Wish Again: Three puffs pay the candles you blow out; each relights at a higher price. The catch: all four burning candles go on the final bill again, at their new prices.
3. The Whole Bunch: Three pops charge connected matching balloons and a bonus for a chain of three or more. The catch: the balloons still standing are part of the set. They get charged too.
4. Plus-Ones Have Plus-Ones: Two spins invite guests, with cover per person and service per full table of three. The catch: every guest brings a plus-one, and all those guests bring another. The guest list quadruples; the extra people and new tables join the bill.
5. Wishful Spending: Pick two wishes. Three spins pay a base gift plus a bonus on a chosen wish; repeat matches double that bonus. The catch: the birthday wish doubles the entire bill, including the two ordinary spins before this game.
6. Reserved For Everyone: Three spins charge a chair and pass its value to its two neighbors. The catch: every chair still at the table is on your reservation. All remaining chair prices are added together and charged once.
7. Confetti Cleanup: Three cash spins fill one popper. Each unit of cash also loads one confetti ticket. Pull the cord: the cash comes out, followed by a cleaning charge for every ticket. The piece count and editable fee are shown in the reveal.
8. Wrapping Not Included: Spin for one to six feet of ribbon, initially quoted at the base price per foot. The catch: the price tag says per inch. Every foot unfolds into twelve priced inches, making the ribbon bill twelve times the quote.
9. Say Fees!: Three photos print digits one to six. They first look like three small amounts added together. The catch: the plus signs vanish and the photos slide into the largest possible three-digit bill, multiplied by the editable number rate.
10. Birthday Discount*: Spin a gift price, then a coupon marked 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent off. The catch: the coupon flips from OFF to ON. That percentage is added to the gift price. The bill never decreases; amounts round to the nearest cent.

STREAM VIEW / PRIVATE CONTROLS
Stream setup creates two private links: controls and transparent viewer.
The viewer runs the game and contains no operator buttons or running totals.
Open one viewer only, then the matching controls. Closing/reopening controls keeps
that viewer's round. Refreshing or closing the viewer starts a fresh round.
Private links contain the room key: keep both complete links private.
Browser pairing uses PeerJS/WebRTC and requires an internet connection.
The public GitHub Pages deployment can be used in an OBS/Streamlabs Browser Source.
Use the generated Transparent viewer link, not the homepage.
Viewer size: 800 x 900. Disable source shutdown and automatic scene refresh.
Local practice also supports H to hide/show the controls for a transparent window capture.
