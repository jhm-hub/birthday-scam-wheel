A FULL ROUND
Enter the player name, press New round, and use Spin twice for normal prizes.
The third press reveals one of the enabled birthday games. The earlier cash stays banked.
Use the large button and any choices that appear. The end card shows the final total.
Pause prevents the next action; a spin already moving is allowed to finish.
New round cancels the current game and clears that round's total.
Sound starts off. Enable it with Sound off, then test the audio before streaming.

THE TEN NEW SCAMS
1. Layer Cake Levy: Three layers. Every new layer charges the whole cake again.
2. Trick Candle Club: Blow out candles for cash; they relight at higher values.
3. Balloon Avalanche: Pop a balloon and every connected balloon of its color.
4. The Plus-One Problem: Add guests; each completed table adds another bonus.
5. Wishful Spending: Choose two wishes; repeated matches get a bigger bonus.
6. Musical Shares: A removed chair pays you and shares its value with neighbors.
   After three removals, the richest surviving chair pays a final throne bonus.
7. Party Popper Payday: Three spins put visible cash into one popper.
   Pull the cord to collect everything inside plus the editable birthday bonus.
8. Ribbon Runaround: Unroll ribbon and collect each milestone bonus you cross.
9. Birthday Photo Finish: Print three digits, then arrange them into the bill.
10. Birthday Bingo: Five draws. Every stamp pays; completed lines add bonuses.
    A repeated number moves clockwise to the next empty square.

MAKE IT YOURS
Prices & settings edits game names, amounts, enabled games, spin length, and sound level.
Apply starts a fresh round. Export settings saves a portable backup.
Imported settings stay in the form until Apply is pressed.

STREAM VIEW / PRIVATE CONTROLS
Stream setup creates two private links: controls and transparent viewer.
The viewer runs the game and contains no operator buttons or running totals.
Open one viewer only, then the matching controls. Closing/reopening controls keeps
that viewer's round. Refreshing or closing the viewer starts a fresh round.
Private links contain the room key: keep both complete links private.
Browser pairing uses PeerJS/WebRTC and requires an internet connection.
The public GitHub Pages deployment can be used in an OBS/Streamlabs Browser Source.
Use the generated Transparent viewer link, not the homepage.
Viewer size: 800 x 900. Disable source shutdown and automatic scene refresh.
Local practice also supports H to hide/show the controls for a transparent window capture.
